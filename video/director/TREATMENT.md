# Jay+1 Field Notes — director's treatment (v2: the two-hander heist)

Supersedes the straight field-report treatment. John, on the first cut: "too linear… a declarative video
instead of one that people want to watch." He chose the two-hander with the heist freeze frame.

**Logline.** Fifty-six minutes before submissions close, a man with no project walks into a hackathon, spots
the prize, and ignores his AI partner reading him the clock. Sixty minutes later, the film you are watching is
the heist.

## The two characters (on-screen lines over the shots)

- **JAY** — unbothered. Instrument Serif, paper `#f2efe5`, 112 px, speaker tag `JAY` in Plex Mono muted
  `#c5bcc0`. Short lines. Never explains himself.
- **+1** — the anxious straight man who reads the clock. IBM Plex Mono, coral `#f0a18b`, 40 px, tag `+1`.
  States facts. Is always right and always ignored.
- **The clock** — a coral chip top-right (`2:04 PM`), the third character. It moves only forward, and it
  jumps faster as the film goes on.
- **THE MARK** — the heist gag: freeze frame, flash, punch-in on the DJI box on the HyperFrames table, a coral
  stamp `THE MARK` set at -6°. It hits **three times**, each shorter: 1.2 s, 0.5 s, 0.25 s.

## Sound

John's real voice from `IMG_3528.MOV` carries three beats: the mark (cold open), "let's do a slow cut" (the
montage gag) and the button. Everything else is location room tone plus a dry clock tick under each +1 line
(a sound effect, not a voice). No music under John's voice; a short sting is allowed on the title and the end
card. No other person's voice, ever.

## Why these jokes

- The clock is the +1's only weapon, so it is the only thing that moves on screen when Jay ignores it.
- "Let's do a slow cut" is something John really said on camera. Setting it against the fastest cutting in the
  film is the one joke the footage hands us for free; it must be his real voice.
- THE MARK shrinks each time (1.2 s, 0.5 s, 0.25 s) because the heist speeds up, then comes back slow at
  the button: the job is done.
- Beat 13 is the payoff of beat 3: same frame, same push, fifty-six minutes later. The audience reads the clock
  before they read the line.
- "You're watching it" says the film is the project. "I did most of it" says who made it: one person, plus
  one intelligence.

## Facts this rests on (nothing invented)

Arrived about 2:04 PM; submissions closed at 3:00 PM; no project on arrival; the +1 kept reading him the clock
and he said he didn't care about it; he said "Let's discuss ideas"; MLH's sign-in wouldn't accept that he is
human; the "submit your project" sign is in the footage; the prize is a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 for the best demo
video using HyperFrames. The line pairs "Eyes on the clock / Eyes on the mark" and "I did most of it" are
dramatised banter between the two characters, not claims about the event.
